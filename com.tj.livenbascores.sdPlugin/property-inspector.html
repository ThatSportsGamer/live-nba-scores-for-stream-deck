Tracking:
Opens ESPN Gamecast until the game actually starts, then this URL instead — handy for a regional sports network's live-game page or any other link you'd rather use. Keeps opening it for 30 minutes after the final buzzer, then reverts to Gamecast.
100%
Score and status text is white (or gold/red) — lighter or brighter backgrounds can make it harder to read.
How it works:
• Scores refresh every 30 seconds (every 15 in the last 2:00 of the 4th quarter and overtime)
• Shows score + quarter/clock during live games — the clock turns red in crunch time
• The key flashes in the leading team's color on every lead change
• Shows matchup and tip-off time before the game, and the next game on off days
• Shows Final / Final/OT when the game ends
• Covers NBA, WNBA, and G League — searching an NBA team also finds its G League affiliate
